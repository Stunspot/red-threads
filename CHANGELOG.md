# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-08

Initial public release of RED THREADS Conspiracy Unraveler.

One self-contained skill brings mechanism-led research, source acquisition and ancestry, identity and financial reconciliation, competing explanations, actor games, campaign checkpoints, and correction into a portable case workflow.

The Python toolkit creates and validates case format 1, generates an offline atlas, exports tables and JSON, traces correction impact, surfaces structural candidates, ranks recorded leads, resumes checkpoints, and applies guarded revisions to new files. The atlas connects network, timeline, explanations, leads, games, and sources, with editorial curation and saved views.

The release includes the fictional Meridian case, customer guides, source provenance, and split licenses. Local tests and extracted-package operations were exercised, alongside an unfamiliar fictional-record inquiry and separate correction/resume continuation. Fresh-host installation/discovery and live-web accuracy remain unverified; see [evidence and scope](documentation/evidence-and-licenses.md).

There is no earlier public case-format migration. Keep your own cases outside the installed skill and retain prior case revisions when upgrading.
