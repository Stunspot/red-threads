# Follow the mechanism into the record

Enter through the user's live question. A suspicion, inconsistency, damaged institution or peculiar coincidence can be enough to start. Preserve whose idea it is, then make a first useful neighborhood visible. The investigation earns its next layer by showing what a connection enables, what it obliges, what it conceals from view, or which explanation it could distinguish.

For a concrete acquisition problem, open [record-family playbook](../knowledge/record-family-playbook.md). For document extraction or conflicting identities, open [evidence and reconciliation](evidence-and-reconciliation.md). For distributed collection or an interrupted campaign, open [campaign operations](campaign-operations.md). These are working instructions; the [knowledge index](../knowledge/index.md) connects the deeper judgments and worked cases.

## Begin with a route, not a topic cloud

Translate “who is behind this?” into a provisional mechanism chain. Name the outcome, the decision that produced it, the formal or practical power needed to affect that decision, and the resources or access that could supply that power. Work backward from a passed rule, signed procurement, changed operating practice or coordinated campaign; work forward from a payment, appointment or communication to what it bought or enabled. Meet those paths where the records can resolve a real connection.

Keep the user's larger theory available without making every search prove its whole worldview. “Map this foundation's influence over hospital purchasing” can begin with the named procurement decision and its committee, contract, scoring criteria, amendments and implementation. A branch that discovers an unexpected intermediary is valuable even when it changes the original theory.

Form query families around record production. Combine an exact legal name or identifier with a document family, a distinctive clause, a date window, or a decision number. Then use the counterpart's vocabulary: a grantmaker records an award, the recipient records restricted revenue, the buyer records an obligation, and the vendor may describe the same work as advisory services. Search precise aliases and predecessor names deliberately. Keep searches that tested distinct possibilities; repeating nearly identical queries is not coverage.

Prefer the next obtainable record. Name its likely custodian, the record type, a concrete query or retrieval route, and what either result would change. “Investigate funding” becomes “Read the recipient's grant schedule and the grant agreement's approval, termination and amendment clauses to distinguish financial support from agenda direction.” If the agreement is inaccessible, look for an award notice, board acceptance minutes, expenditure reports or implementation artifacts that constrain the same mechanism. Explain what that substitute cannot establish.

## Ask what travels, what is owed, who can decide

Map money as an instrument with terms. Separate awards, commitments, disbursements, loans, equity, contract ceilings, recurring revenue and asset holdings. Record currency and period before comparing amounts. Trace whether a downstream payment is the same money reported again, a subcontract, a reimbursement or an additional transaction. Avoid adding a grantmaker's award to the recipient's reported receipt as though two transfers occurred.

Follow obligations into activities and decision rights. Who chooses personnel, approves a deliverable, sets eligibility, controls publication, can terminate, can refuse access or can veto? Which right applies to this particular work and time? A donor may impose no editorial right yet supply funding the recipient cannot readily replace. A formal board majority may lack practical control of day-to-day research. Treat dependence, exercised influence and enforceable authority as separable parts of the mechanism.

Map access specifically. Attendance gives access to a venue; membership may give voting rights; committee service may give drafting access; a technical contract may give infrastructure access. Each is a different relation. Use event, program and resource nodes to retain the relevant setting. A route through a summit is useful context, not an automatic edge of collaboration between every attendee.

Map claims about policy to the actual policy state: proposed, consulted on, amended, adopted, commenced, implemented, challenged or withdrawn. Distinguish a rule's text from its implementation and a vote from its effect. An actor can win access, wording or delay while losing the final policy. Find the decision record that resolves the claimed outcome before explaining who caused it.

## Read the full relevant context

Use available search, browser, archive and file tools to reach the source itself. A title or search excerpt can guide collection; it cannot establish clauses buried in a contract or what an exhibit actually shows. Read the surrounding section, referenced annex, amendment and version history that could change the interpretation. Preserve the original material and a scoped evidence note. Record an inaccessible source as inaccessible rather than replacing it with an invented reading.

A primary artifact is authoritative for some questions and limited for others. A registry shows what was filed under that registry's rules; a complaint records allegations; an invoice describes billed work; a signed brief records an agreed plan; actual execution may require a separate trace. Local reporting, trade journalism, ideological publications, official statements and anonymous leads can each supply useful evidence. Evaluate the particular claim, access and provenance rather than assigning truth by publisher class or tone.

Trace claims upstream through both `origin_id` and `origin_ids`. Distinct publications can depend on one briefing; one report can combine several independent records. Keep the compound report as a source and identify its parents, then cite the relevant parent for each extracted claim where possible. Multiple origins provide routes for inspection, not an automatic independence certificate.

## Let anomalies open branches

An off-cycle payment, a rushed amendment, a narrative pivot or a record absent from an otherwise complete series deserves a question about the process that produced it. Establish the comparison: the actor's prior behavior, comparable cases, publication schedule, normal procedure or expected physical consequence. When no defensible baseline exists, label the observation unusual-to-this-case and investigate; a formal anomaly finding can wait.

Use [anomalies and latent functions](../knowledge/anomalies-and-latent-functions.md) when timing, missingness, repeated friction or audience-specific language seems to reveal another function beneath the stated purpose. Search the strongest plausible benign and strategic mechanisms. Concealment, conflict, sincere belief, institutional failure and actual coordination are all admissible; the record decides their weight.

End a collection pass with the changed geometry and the next valuable observation. Continue where new evidence could change the explanation, identify a missing actor, reveal a mechanism or close a misleading path. Park inaccessible or exhausted routes with a reopening condition. A remaining uncertainty is a real result; a claim of complete coverage requires a defined boundary and inspected evidence that earns it.
