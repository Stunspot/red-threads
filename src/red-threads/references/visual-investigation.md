# Investigate through the geometry

Build a view that lets the user notice something consequential, inspect it and ask a better question. Start with the live inquiry's smallest useful neighborhood. Keep the wider network available as context. The atlas projects `case.json`; filters, layout, path traces and structural scenarios change the presentation, not the evidence. Use [worked investigations](../knowledge/worked-investigations.md) when a view needs to become a concrete collection or reasoning move.

## Compose the map around the question

For “Who can make this happen?”, show resources, obligations and decision rights. Follow the grant condition, appointment right, procedural veto or infrastructure dependency that could carry influence. For “Why does everyone say this?”, show source ancestry and where apparently separate accounts begin. For “What changed?”, show effective intervals and decision stages. For “Which explanation survives?”, compare support, challenge and the next discriminating observation. For “What might they do next?”, show the actor game with assumptions beside conditional consequences.

Use the smallest view that exposes the mechanism. An overview reveals neighborhoods and bridges; an actor focus reveals a particular dependency; a short path reveals a chain whose missing joint can be named. The whole graph need not fit legibly on one screen. A huge web with no readable verbs is a collection index awaiting an investigative composition.

Curate recurring overview choices in `case.views`: `group_order` arranges the user's domains; `group_hubs` names useful anchors; `overview` explicitly selects and orders the visible nodes of each group; `pinned_nodes` keeps case-relevant anchors in view. The automatic `overview_limit` controls automatic selection, not an explicit editorial list. Preserve supplied grouping and order when it serves the user's question. A hub designation or central placement is editorial organization, not a measurement of control. Group membership records an organizing choice, not an established alliance.

If the built-in atlas modes do not expose the relationship clearly, make the earned additional diagram: a decision-rights ladder, nested ownership tree, document version comparison, two-clock sequence, money reconciliation, dependency chain, audience-by-message matrix or conditional game tree. Link its objects back to case IDs and state what its arrows mean. Keep canonical evidence in the case model; supplementary diagrams are projections with their own declared scope.

## Let the visual grammar carry the epistemic grammar

Every asserted link needs its verb, direction, status, time and inspectable basis. Keep documented, reported, inferred, hypothesized, disputed and retracted relations distinguishable. A documented payment can sit beside a speculative influence account; proximity cannot promote the latter. Show uncertainty where the eye reaches the claim, including in any prose explaining a highlighted route.

Read a path in its real verbs: A funded B; B employed C; C attended D. Then name what would be needed to conclude that A directed D. A common summit is an event node, not a clique of collaboration edges. A shared service provider may be a broker, common infrastructure or campaign coordinator; the contract and conduct decide which. Edge width cannot turn a contract ceiling into a disbursement, and node size cannot turn a well-covered actor into the most powerful actor.

Unknown dates remain visible in a time slice unless explicitly excluded. Explain open-ended intervals and imprecise dates. An appointment with no recorded end is not verified current employment. Retractions remain inspectable even when omitted from the active default. Money comparisons require compatible kinds, currencies and periods; label estimates and coverage gaps before showing totals.

Make the geometry readable: sufficient spacing for labels, distinct arrow destinations, legible selected paths, clear layer and status keys, useful keyboard focus and a usable narrow view. Dense regions may need focus, grouping or a separate panel. A label should not require guessing which node it belongs to.

## Convert what becomes visible into the next lead

| Visible pattern | Investigative interpretation to test | Concrete next move |
|---|---|---|
| One apparent resource hub | Scarce supply, ordinary brokerage or unusually good collection coverage | Read substitution, concentration and decision-right records; compare undercovered peers |
| A bridge between sectors | A possible transmission route | Identify what crosses it, under which obligation, and who can refuse or replace it |
| Many agreeing publications | Independent access, copied origins or a common communications process | Inspect upstream artifacts and recover claim-specific source ancestry |
| A grant and appointment precede a decision | A temporally possible mechanism | Read terms effective before the draft and the decision's actual amendment history |
| A cluster vanishes in documented-only view | Theory depends on tentative links | Inspect the specific tentative bridge and choose its nearest obtainable record |
| An empty interval or missing counterpart | Event absence, record absence, access failure or search gap | Establish expected observability and inspect an independent custodian |

The `scan` command can surface structural candidates and coverage prompts. Treat them as questions over the supplied graph. Compare the broader graph with documented-only and relevant date slices. A candidate that disappears reveals sensitivity to a claim status or time assumption; a candidate that persists remains structural, not a finding of wrongdoing. Investigative value comes from identifying which missing observation would change the interpretation.

Turn a discovery into a lead with a record, custodian, query, expected observation and meaningful alternative result. “The bridge looks central” becomes “Read the service agreement to distinguish exclusive approval from ordinary distribution, then seek one comparable client.” “These reports have separate URLs” becomes “Read their stated provenance to determine whether they share the same witness.” Save the lead, not merely the screenshot that inspired it.

## Separate structural scenarios from evidential withdrawal

Remove a node to see how visible connectivity changes. Label the result as a structural scenario under this graph's coverage. A route disappearing does not prove real-world indispensability; an alternative path does not prove actual substitution. Ask what capacity, rights, timing and costs make a replacement feasible. Record an explicit hypothetical game if useful, without rewriting observed relations.

Source impact asks a different question: which recorded judgments require review if material changes? Follow source ancestry and declared dependencies through edges, nodes, hypotheses, games and leads. Inspect surviving support before revising. A multi-origin synthesis can lose one parent's claim while retaining another. A corrected hypothesis can change a game even when the actor network is unchanged. The command reports the declared frontier; consequential premises left only in prose require manual review. It does not automatically retract claims.

Keep those changes visible across linked views. Selecting a source should lead to its provenance and dependent objects; selecting a hypothesis should reveal its actual support and challenges; selecting a game should expose the premise and next signals. Navigation is useful when the user can reach the weak joint without reverse-engineering the data model.

## Render, inspect and preserve the working view

Use the bundled offline atlas when available. Generate to a new explicit destination, open it through an allowed host route and inspect the composition that carries the finding. Check the decisive label, selected route, evidence panel and relevant time/status handling. Repair a specific defect in spacing, grouping, labels or selection, then inspect that affected view. Source text and local note paths remain inert; the renderer does not fetch evidence. Respect any host browser restriction and use an available permitted alternative without disguising a blocked route.

When rendering is unavailable, deliver the useful textual neighborhood or suitable diagram with canonical case data and the exact display limitation. State which interaction was actually inspected. A successful render command alone does not establish that the intended geometry is readable.

Save a view snapshot to preserve filters, selection and structural scenarios, and load it only against the matching case. A saved view is presentation state; `case.views` is canonical editorial configuration; evidence revisions belong in case updates. A later evidence revision can make a prior view incomplete, so pair it with the relevant case revision. After a substantial pass, report what became visible, what remains unresolved and the next discriminating record. Carry decisive evidence into prose so the user need not click every node to understand the finding.
