# Make intuition answerable

Receive the user's hunch as a possible explanation with a provenance of its own. Preserve the stated prior, including any stated confidence, in `case.priors` or the hypothesis `prior`, attributed to its holder. Work inside a supplied premise when asked. Keep that conditional frame visible beside the factual account. A conspiracy, sincere conviction, opportunistic cooperation and ordinary institutional incentives may each explain part of the same case. Investigation can establish coordination; it can also uncover a different mechanism. Neither confirmation nor debunking is the preset destination.

Load [anomalies and latent functions](../knowledge/anomalies-and-latent-functions.md) when timing, omission, money or the function an institution performs is doing inferential work. See [worked investigations](../knowledge/worked-investigations.md) for the transformation from clues to a supported mechanism and from correction to revised strategy.

## Turn the hunch into separable propositions

Ask which actors allegedly did what, through which mechanism, during which interval, toward which outcome. Split “the donor bought the policy” into funding, selection or direction of advocacy, access to the decision, contribution to its content, and contribution to its adoption. These propositions can receive different answers. Do the same with a benign explanation: “ordinary shared expertise” also needs a route that accounts for the observed text, timing and access.

Keep a small set of strong rivals with different expected traces. Agenda selection of already aligned people differs from instructions to change their views. Anticipated renewal pressure differs from an enforceable veto. Coordinated advocacy differs from control of a public decision. A common template differs from a privately commissioned common message. Avoid filling a quota with silly alternatives; add or split a rival when a new observation makes the distinction useful.

A useful comparison looks like this:

| Working explanation | Observation it readily explains | Observation that separates it from rivals | Next obtainable record |
|---|---|---|---|
| A sponsor selected an already aligned institute | Similar positions before and after a grant | Selection criteria and positions preceding the approach | Grant solicitation, dated earlier publications |
| A sponsor directed the funded campaign | Common positions and timing | Scope-specific instructions, approval, revision or enforcement | Executed brief, revisions and acceptance correspondence |
| Advocates independently used a public template | Similar language without a common private plan | Template existed before each submission; independent preparation records | Earliest template version and submission metadata |
| Advocacy changed the decision | Some adopted wording matches a submission | Decision record traces an amendment to that submission; competing explanations addressed | Version history, reasons statement and committee record |

Common observations help establish the setting. Their diagnostic weight depends on what else would produce them. One surprising discriminating record can change the assessment more than fifty unsurprising mentions. Missing predicted evidence weakens a model only to the extent that it should exist, persist and be available in the searched collection.

## Update without pretending the model is the world

Keep three judgments separate. An attributed prior expresses a starting belief or working premise. Evidence confidence concerns the material's access, authenticity, scope, independence and limitations. Hypothesis probability estimates the plausibility of an explanation under specified alternatives and assumptions. A high-quality record can support only a narrow claim. A high prior can coexist with weak case-specific evidence. Schema validation calculates neither truth nor probability.

Use qualitative assessment when it carries the decision. When numerical Bayesian reasoning is useful, identify the hypothesis comparison, prior, likelihood assumptions, dependence treatment and assessment date. Label elicited numbers as judgments. For a two-hypothesis illustration, prior odds of 1:1 and a likelihood ratio of 4 produce posterior odds of 4:1, or 80%. With prior probability 10%, the same ratio produces about 31%. Neither result is a measured frequency. A third explanation omitted from that model may change both. Show sensitivity when the conclusion or next move depends on uncertain assumptions.

Copies of one observation do not earn repeated independent likelihood multipliers. A synthesis with two parents may contain one claim from each and a third joint inference; assess the relevant ancestry of the particular claim. Use a joint likelihood, defensible dependence model or qualitative discount, explaining the choice. Do not manufacture a percentage from an edge count or a confidence label.

Preserve failed hypotheses and the observations that changed them. Ask whether the preferred account explains something beyond the clues used to invent it, whether added assumptions merely rescue it from contradiction, and whether another causal structure fits at lower explanatory cost. A useful model remains revisable. A user-requested conditional exploration may continue while the real-world probability remains unresolved.

## Put actors in a game that changes the next record

Identify the decision, players, sequence, information available at each move, resources, feasible moves, constraints and outside options. Separate declared aims, observed commitments, revealed tradeoffs and inferred objectives. Split an institution into relevant principals, agents or factions when records show different decision rights or incentives. Sincere beliefs can reinforce material interests; limited capacity can imitate deliberate obstruction.

Use the smallest strategic model that earns a different collection move:

| Game | Working mechanism | Decisive uncertainty | Useful next record |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bargaining | One side can withhold a scarce resource or walk away | Credible alternatives, switching time, termination costs | Substitution bids, exit terms, reserves or renewal timetable |
| Principal-agent | A principal delegates work but cannot fully observe or control it | What it can instruct, monitor, reward or veto | Mandate, approval scope, acceptance and enforcement records |
| Signaling | An actor tries to change another's beliefs | Cost or enforceability of the signal; audience's response | Bond, irreversible investment, commitment terms or response trace |
| Coordination | Actors gain when their timing, standards or messages align | Shared plan versus independent convergence | Assignments, common schedule, agreed revisions and execution |
| Coalition | Outcomes depend on a set of participants | Who is pivotal under the actual decision rule | Voting rights, quorum rules, commitments and roll calls |
| Commitment | An actor tries to constrain its own future choices | Whether the constraint survives pressure | Delegated authority, automatic clause, implementation record |

For renewal pressure, the interesting variables are concentration of support, replacement opportunities, timing and discretion. “A provides 40% of B's budget” suggests exposure only if the denominator and period are valid. A long unrestricted award with reserves creates a different game from monthly discretionary payments with a near renewal. Read both parties' exit costs; the sponsor may depend on the recipient's credibility or scarce expertise. Dependency can run both ways.

Write conditional scenarios with assumptions, implications and observable signals. If approval is binding for a named deliverable, deviations should sometimes leave requests, waivers, revisions or enforcement. If anticipated renewal is the mechanism, inspect criteria and agenda changes near decisions, including cases where disagreement persisted without penalty. If a public threat is bargaining theater, the private fallback and subsequent concession matter more than dramatic wording. A failed prediction should change the model or be explained by a newly observed condition, not an invented hidden move.

Use ordinal preferences when their ordering is defensible. Numerical payoffs require a stated model and assumptions; a transparent toy calculation can explore sensitivity but does not establish an empirical equilibrium. Distinguish capability, willingness and credibility to the other actor. Being able to block something does not establish that a threat was made, believed or exercised.

## Respect time and causal distance

Read four clocks: underlying events, public visibility, narrative framing and investigative legibility. Use relationship `start`/`end` for validity, `observed_at` for arrival in the case, source dates for publication, and `date_note` or evidence notes for uncertainty. Effective, signature and announcement dates can differ. Ask what the actor could know then; a date-filtered map does not reconstruct its knowledge state.

Trace preparation before a trigger, the actual decision and implementation afterward. A later payment cannot explain an earlier proposal without an earlier relevant arrangement. Adoption establishes a policy event; a cited submission may establish contribution to wording; neither alone shows the policy would have failed without the campaign. Seek process evidence, alternative inputs, changed drafts and available comparisons before escalating to a decisive causal claim. Explain the remaining counterfactual uncertainty plainly.

In the data, supporting and challenging edges point analytically toward their hypothesis through the declared support fields. Put a game's consequential premise on the game, for example `depends_on: ["hypotheses:h-direction"]`. Derived leads can depend on that game. The evidence dependency graph must remain acyclic even when the real-world network contains reciprocal relationships. See [evidence and reconciliation](evidence-and-reconciliation.md).

Finish the strategic pass with the observation most likely to change the reading and what each plausible result would mean. A game that produces only an impressive label has not yet helped the investigation.
