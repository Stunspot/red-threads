# Knowledge that enters when the investigation needs it

RED THREADS works through a live case, not a compulsory reading sequence. Load the smallest playbook that changes the present judgment or next action. A short connection question may need only a source, a precise relation and a useful neighborhood. A sustained investigation earns acquisition notes, rival explanations, strategic models and a checkpoint as the work develops.

## Choose by the decision in front of you

## Use the worked transformations deliberately

Worked case 1 begins with funding, co-attendance and copied reporting, then acquires a signed plan, exercised approval and execution records. The result positively establishes coordinated advocacy. A separate decision record establishes contribution to wording while leaving decisive impact unresolved. Load it when “connection,” “coordination,” “control” and “caused the policy” are sliding together in either direction.

Worked case 2 starts with delay and missingness. Record definitions and decision rights change the map, revealing a real function that may arise from capacity limits, divided authority or deliberate exploitation. Load it when intuition points toward hidden machinery but the current account jumps from who benefits to who intended it.

Worked case 3 replaces a broad draft clause with an executed narrow one. It shows how a correction travels through multiple origins, authority descriptions, edges, hypotheses, games and leads while independent coordination evidence survives. Load it when a revised premise must change the whole investigation coherently.

These examples are fictional instruction, not source material for real cases or substitutes for unfamiliar-case performance. Their value is the transformation to repeat, not their actors, wording or predetermined answers.

## Keep the knowledge subordinate to the work

Read the relevant source family and reasoning passage, then return to the actual evidence. The bundled playbooks are operational adaptations of selected investigative canon chapters, with exact source lineage in provenance. They do not assert that every missing document indicates concealment, every common intermediary coordinates clients, or every public record tells the whole truth. They also do not require an investigator to suspend a supported conclusion merely because a larger theory remains open.

If the next step is obvious and authorized, do it. When evidence is unavailable, preserve that precise gap and a meaningful substitute or reopening condition. When a new observation changes the mechanism, update the case and the geometry. The reading has earned its place when the investigator sees a better joint to pull.
