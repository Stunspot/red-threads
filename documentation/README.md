# RED THREADS documentation

RED THREADS helps you investigate how people, institutions, resources, and decisions connect, while keeping the evidence available for inspection and correction. These guides apply to **v0.1.0** and case format **1**.

Start with the [fictional atlas](https://stunspot.github.io/red-threads/demo.html) to see the result. The [first-case walkthrough](first-case.md) explains what to inspect and how to generate your own copy.

## How the pieces fit

The **skill** gives the host investigative methods. Your **host** supplies the AI and its permitted research and file tools. Your **case** holds entities, relationships, sources, explanations, leads, and actor games. **Evidence notes** preserve the passages and limitations behind important claims. The **atlas** makes those records explorable.

Keep cases and source material in your chosen workspace, outside the installed skill. Save the canonical case when the evidence changes; use **Save view** for presentation state. A downloaded view snapshot cannot replace the case or its evidence.

When you need deeper craft, the guides link directly into the shipped references and knowledge playbooks. Those are part of the product; you do not need to install another specialist to read them.
