# Evidence, scope, and licenses

RED THREADS v0.1.0 is a single self-contained skill with investigative instructions, knowledge playbooks, deterministic case tools, and an offline atlas. Its useful claim is an inspectable workflow: follow evidence into mechanisms, compare explanations, and preserve the case for correction and resumption.

## What has been exercised

Local release checks ran 52 tests: 51 passed and one Windows symlink-privilege test was skipped, with no failures. They exercise Python data and CLI operations and actual bundled JavaScript geometry, dependency, and saved-state logic. Two independently identified view-save defects were repaired and their regression probes passed.

A clean extraction of the release ZIP successfully validated, rendered, scanned, ranked leads, and resumed the fictional Meridian case. A separate operator also acquired and reconciled unfamiliar supplied fictional records. A fresh continuation performed correction impact, guarded revision, and resumption. These exercises support the documented local workflow.

The public [tests](../tests/) and [release builder](../tools/build_release.py) let maintainers repeat the reproducible portions. The private evaluation records are not customer data or public cargo.

## What those checks do not establish

The fictional exercises do not measure live-web research accuracy or provide a numerical performance rating. This release has not been installed and discovered on a fresh Codex or Claude host. Account availability and host permissions can change whether a particular tool or installation route is usable.

CLI rendering and JavaScript tests do not by themselves establish browser, keyboard, screen-reader, or representative-user success. The [documentation project state](project.json) records the review methods and remaining boundaries. No formal accessibility conformance claim is made.

Validation checks structure and declared evidence boundaries. It does not verify sources, reconcile every identity, judge truth, or prove complete dependency coverage. The atlas's geography is editorial. Its structural removal and path tools inspect the supplied graph; they do not calculate guilt, influence, or causal necessity.

## Authorship and source lineage

Created by Sam “stunspot” Walker / Collaborative Dynamics, with product construction and public writing by Nova. RED THREADS selectively adapts investigative methods from **Stunspot's Guide to Investigative News Intelligence**. The [provenance register](../src/red-threads/references/provenance.md) identifies its source revision, relevant sections, operational transformations, and exclusions.

The runtime references and worked investigations were composed for this product. It does not bundle the full investigative canon or another whole Augment. ICE was consulted in design; its persona is not included or required. The host keeps the caller's identity.
